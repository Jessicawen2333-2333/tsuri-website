Personal Insurance

Protecting you and the people who depend on you.

Life does not come with a warning. Personal insurance ensures that if something unexpected happens to you, your family's future stays intact. I help you find the right cover for your stage of life, whether you have just bought your first home, started a family, or simply want peace of mind knowing you are protected.

Life insurance is designed to provide a financial safety net for your loved ones if the worst should happen. If you pass away while the policy is active, your chosen beneficiaries receive a tax-free lump sum, ensuring they are not forced to make difficult financial decisions during an already emotional time.

1. Financial Security for Your Family

The primary benefit is the lump-sum death benefit paid directly to your nominated beneficiaries. This ensures the people you love have the financial breathing room they need without making urgent decisions under pressure.

2. Debt & Mortgage Protection

  • Clear the mortgage: Ensure your family can stay in their home without the burden of monthly repayments
  • Settle debts: Pay off personal loans, credit cards, or car finance so your estate remains intact
  • Business obligations: Protect your business from debt exposure triggered by your passing

3. Income Replacement

If your family relies on your income, Life Insurance acts as a backup. It can cover:

  • Day-to-day living expenses including groceries, utilities, and transport
  • Future education costs and university fees for children
  • Childcare or ongoing household support

4. Terminal Illness Benefit

Most modern policies include a Terminal Illness Benefit. If you are diagnosed with a terminal condition (typically 12 months or less life expectancy), your sum insured is paid out early so you can:

  • Focus on your health and family without financial pressure
  • Cover specialist medical costs or palliative care
  • Arrange your affairs and make memories with loved ones

5. Funeral & Bereavement Cover

Many policies offer an advance payout (typically $15,000 to $20,000) within 24 to 48 hours of a claim to help cover funeral costs, legal fees, and immediate travel for family members.

6. Why Act Now?

  • Locked-in rates: Premiums are lower and easier to secure when you are younger and healthy
  • Guaranteed insurability: Waiting can mean exclusions or declined applications due to health changes
  • Complete peace of mind: Knowing your family is protected allows you to live more fully today

Trauma insurance pays you a tax-free lump sum if you are diagnosed with a specific, life-altering medical condition. Unlike Health Insurance which covers medical bills, this payout is yours to use however you need. The goal is simple: give you financial breathing room so you can focus on recovery, not finances.

What Is Typically Covered

Most policies cover 40 or more major medical conditions, including:

  • Cancer (including early-stage cancer benefit)
  • Heart attack and coronary artery surgery
  • Stroke
  • Major organ transplant
  • Multiple sclerosis and neurological conditions
  • Alzheimer's disease and dementia
  • Loss of limbs, sight, or speech
  • Intensive care confinement

Why Choose Trauma Cover?

  • Complete flexibility: Use the funds for your mortgage, private treatment, rehabilitation, or supporting your family
  • Bridge the income gap: If you or your partner needs extended time off work, the lump sum maintains your lifestyle
  • Access to better care: Fund private treatment, home modifications, or therapies not publicly funded
  • Worldwide cover: Whether at home, travelling, or overseas, your cover remains active

Optional Benefits Available

  • Early Cancer Upgrade: Covers several minor, treatable cancer conditions at early diagnosis, paying a partial benefit before the condition progresses
  • Trauma Buy-Back: Reinstates your level of cover after a claim without requiring further medical information
  • Total Permanent Disablement condition: An additional claimable condition covering permanent disability due to accident or illness

Your ability to earn is your most valuable asset. If illness or injury prevents you from working, Income Protection provides regular monthly payments replacing a portion of your income so you can meet your financial commitments and protect your lifestyle while you recover.

Three Cover Options

  • Agreed Value Cover: Benefit payments are based on an amount agreed when the policy is taken out. Maximum 62.5% of your taxable income. Provides certainty.
  • Indemnity Cover: Benefit based on your income at the time of claim. Maximum 75% of your taxable income.
  • Loss of Earnings Cover: Calculated as either agreed value or indemnity, whichever is higher. Ideal if your income fluctuates.

Key Benefits

  • Maintain your lifestyle: Keep up with rent, school fees, and groceries without drawing on long-term savings
  • Rehabilitation support: Many policies include Return to Work benefits, funding retraining or equipment to assist recovery
  • Partial disability: If you return to work part-time, the policy can top up your reduced earnings
  • 24/7 coverage: Unlike ACC, Income Protection covers illness and injury anywhere, anytime

How It Works

Important: ACC only covers accidents. Income Protection covers both accidents and illnesses, including cancer, heart conditions, and mental health conditions, wherever and whenever they occur.

Mortgage and Rent Protection provides a monthly payment to help replace lost income or cover essential housing costs if illness or injury prevents you from working. It is designed to keep a roof over your family's head no matter what happens.

What It Covers

  • Monthly mortgage repayments while you are unable to work
  • Rental payments for those who rent rather than own
  • General living expenses to supplement lost income
  • Cover for both illness and accident, not just ACC-eligible events

Why It Matters

  • Your home is often your most important asset. This cover ensures you never have to choose between your health and keeping it
  • Bridges the gap between your sick leave running out and your ability to return to work
  • Often combined with Income Protection for comprehensive cover
  • Works alongside ACC payments, with benefit adjusted accordingly

Note: Redundancy Cover can only be chosen in combination with Income Cover or Mortgage Repayment Cover, providing a monthly payment for up to 6 months if you are made involuntarily redundant.

Total and Permanent Disablement (TPD) insurance provides a one-time lump sum payment if you become so disabled through illness or injury that you are unlikely to ever work again. It is a financial safety net designed to help you navigate a new way of living.

What the Lump Sum Can Cover

  • Paying off your mortgage so you own your home outright
  • Ongoing medical and rehabilitation costs
  • Home modifications to accommodate your disability
  • Long-term care and support costs
  • Providing for your family's future financial security
  • Repaying any outstanding debts or loans

How It Differs from Income Protection

  • Income Protection pays monthly while you recover. It is designed for a temporary inability to work
  • Disability Benefit (TPD) pays a one-off lump sum for permanent, total disablement when you will not return to work
  • Both can be held simultaneously for comprehensive layered protection
  • TPD can be structured as standalone cover or as an add-on to Life Insurance

Redundancy Insurance pays a monthly amount for up to 6 months if you are made involuntarily redundant. It gives you a financial back-up plan so you can focus on your next career step without scrambling to cover living expenses.

Key Details

  • Covers involuntary redundancy only, not voluntary resignation
  • Monthly payments for up to 6 months
  • Can cover mortgage repayments, bills, and everyday living costs
  • Must be selected alongside Income Cover or Mortgage Repayment Cover
  • Provides breathing room to find the right next role without financial pressure

Who Should Consider This?

  • Anyone with a mortgage or significant fixed financial commitments
  • Those in industries or roles where restructuring is possible
  • Single-income households where one redundancy has an immediate financial impact
  • Those who want a comprehensive protection package rather than single-risk cover

Medical Insurance

Access the treatment you need, without the wait or the worry.

New Zealand's public health system is under significant pressure. Medical Insurance gives you access to private specialists, diagnostics, and treatment when you need it, not when the public waitlist allows. I help you choose cover that genuinely matches your health profile and budget.

Cancer Care Benefit provides comprehensive cover for cancer treatment costs, giving you access to the best available care without the financial stress of navigating private treatment costs on top of a diagnosis.

What Is Covered

  • Surgery related to cancer diagnosis and treatment
  • Chemotherapy and radiation therapy
  • Specialist and oncologist consultations
  • Hormone therapy and targeted drug treatments
  • Early-stage cancer diagnosis benefit
  • Hospital accommodation during treatment
  • Medicines not funded by Pharmac

Why It Matters

  • Cancer is the leading cause of critical illness claims for women in New Zealand
  • Private cancer treatment can cost tens of thousands of dollars. Cancer Care removes that barrier
  • Faster access to diagnosis and treatment. Time is critical with cancer
  • Access to treatments and medications not always funded through the public system

Specialist and Diagnostic Cover removes the waiting time and cost barriers to getting answers. Rather than waiting months for a public referral, this cover allows you to see a private specialist and get diagnostic tests done when you need them.

What Is Covered

  • Private specialist consultations
  • MRI and CT scans
  • X-rays and ultrasounds
  • Pathology and blood work
  • Specialist-recommended diagnostic procedures

The Real Value

  • Early diagnosis dramatically improves treatment outcomes, especially for cancer and cardiac conditions
  • Removes the financial barrier to getting answers quickly
  • Often the first step before treatment is needed. Catching issues early saves cost and improves recovery
  • Avoids months on a public waitlist for a referral that leads to more waiting

Private Medical Insurance provides comprehensive cover for a wide range of medical procedures and hospital stays, giving you full access to private hospital care, your choice of specialist, and faster treatment timelines than the public system.

What Is Typically Covered

  • Private hospital accommodation and surgical procedures
  • Specialist consultations and follow-up care
  • Anaesthetist and surgical assistant fees
  • Diagnostic tests ordered by a specialist
  • Prescription medicines related to treatment
  • Rehabilitation following surgery or illness

Why Private Cover Makes Sense

  • Choose your own specialist rather than being allocated one
  • Avoid lengthy public waitlists for elective and non-urgent procedures
  • Private hospital environments typically support a better recovery experience
  • Can be structured around your budget with various excess options
  • Protects your ACC and public health entitlements for genuine emergencies

Business Insurance

Keep your business running, no matter what happens to you.

As a business owner, you are the engine. If something happens to you, everything you have built is at risk. I work with SME owners to structure cover that protects the business, its people, and its future, built to hold up when a claim needs to be made.

Key Person Insurance protects your business against the financial consequences of losing a crucial team member through serious illness, permanent disability, or death. It safeguards business continuity when the person most critical to your operations is unable to continue.

What It Covers

  • Loss of revenue directly attributed to the key person
  • Costs of recruiting and training a replacement
  • Repayment of business loans personally guaranteed by the key person
  • Cost of a temporary replacement or specialist consultant
  • Maintaining business momentum during a transition period

Who Qualifies as a Key Person?

  • The business owner or co-founder
  • A top-performing salesperson whose relationships drive revenue
  • A technical specialist whose skills are difficult to replace
  • Any individual whose absence would materially impact business income or operations

From Salesh's experience: I know how important it is to structure Key Person cover correctly. The sum insured, the trigger conditions, and how the policy is owned all affect whether a claim succeeds. I make sure your policy is built to pay out when it needs to.

If you are a business owner, you are the engine room of your company. If illness or injury takes you out of action, the business does not just lose its leader, it loses its momentum. Business Income Continuity replaces the income your business generates while you are unable to work.

How It Works

  • Provides a monthly benefit based on your share of business income
  • Covers inability to work due to illness or injury
  • Benefit period options typically range from 2 years to age 65
  • Can be structured to complement personal Income Protection
  • Payments continue while you are unable to perform your key business duties

Why Business Owners Need This Separately

  • Personal Income Protection covers your salary. If your income comes through the business, the structure matters differently
  • Ensures business obligations and distributions can continue without drawing on reserves
  • Protects your business partners and staff from the financial impact of your absence
  • Can be owned by the business as a potentially deductible expense in some structures

Business Overhead Expense (BOE) Insurance ensures your business stays open and operational while you recover from illness or injury. While personal Income Protection covers your personal expenses, BOE covers the ongoing costs of running the business itself.

Typical Expenses Covered

  • Office or premises rent and utilities
  • Staff wages and salaries
  • Business loan repayments
  • Equipment leases and subscriptions
  • Accountancy and professional service fees
  • Business insurance premiums

Key Considerations

  • Designed for sole traders, business owners, and partners in a firm
  • Benefit period is typically 12 to 24 months, giving you time to recover or arrange a handover
  • Works alongside personal Income Protection for full personal and business coverage
  • Premiums are generally tax deductible as a business expense

Shareholder Protection Insurance (also known as Buy-Sell Cover) provides the funding for remaining owners to purchase a departing shareholder's stake, ensuring a smooth and equitable business transition when a shareholder dies or suffers a critical illness.

What It Solves

  • Without this cover, a deceased shareholder's stake passes to their estate, potentially to people with no interest in the business
  • Surviving shareholders may not have liquid capital to buy out a departing partner
  • Provides certainty for all shareholders about what happens in a worst-case scenario
  • Typically structured alongside a buy-sell agreement to be legally enforceable

How It Works

  • Each shareholder takes out a policy on the others, or the company takes policies on all shareholders
  • On a trigger event (death, terminal illness, or critical illness), the payout funds the share purchase
  • The departing shareholder's family receives fair value; the business retains full ownership within the existing team
  • Can be structured to cover the full market value of each shareholder's stake

Business Debt Protection delivers a targeted lump sum to clear business debts in defined circumstances, protecting your family from inheriting business liabilities and helping safeguard the future of the business itself.

What It Covers

  • Business loans and overdrafts personally guaranteed by the owner
  • Commercial mortgages on business premises
  • Equipment finance and leases
  • Investor loans or director's loans within the business

Why This Is Critical

  • Many business owners personally guarantee business debt, meaning the bank can pursue family assets if the business cannot repay
  • A lump sum at the point of death or critical illness prevents forced asset sales
  • Allows the business to continue operating debt-free rather than being wound up
  • Gives your family security that they will not face a burden you did not intend them to carry

ACC Cover Plus Extra (CPX) is a flexible, optional ACC product designed for self-employed people. It gives you greater control and certainty over what you receive if an accident stops you from working, rather than relying on a percentage of fluctuating income.

How It Differs from Standard ACC

  • Standard ACC for self-employed is based on previous income, which can result in lower payouts than expected
  • CPX allows you to set a specific weekly compensation amount regardless of income fluctuation
  • Provides certainty about what you will receive rather than a percentage of variable earnings
  • Can reduce levies compared to standard ACC in many cases

Important Limitations to Know

  • ACC, including CPX, only covers accidents, not illness
  • A health diagnosis such as cancer, heart disease, or a mental health condition is not covered by ACC
  • CPX should always be paired with Income Protection insurance for complete coverage
  • I help self-employed clients structure CPX and Income Protection together to avoid gaps or over-insuring

Trade Insurance & ACC

For those who work with their hands and their livelihoods.

Tradies, contractors, and self-employed workers face unique physical risks every day. ACC provides some protection for accidents, but it is rarely the complete answer. I help people in trade-based roles understand exactly what they are covered for and structure supplementary insurance to close the gaps.

Many tradespeople assume ACC has them fully covered. The reality is more nuanced, and understanding the gaps can make the difference between a manageable setback and a financial crisis. I provide a thorough review of your ACC entitlements and help you structure cover that genuinely protects your income and ability to keep working.

What ACC Covers (and Does Not)

  • Covered: Work-related and non-work accidents resulting in physical injury
  • Not covered: Illness, disease, or gradual onset conditions such as back degeneration or repetitive strain
  • Not covered: Mental health conditions not directly linked to a physical accident
  • Limited: ACC pays a maximum of 80% of your pre-injury income, and only for accidents
  • Gap risk: If your income has changed significantly, ACC may calculate payments based on outdated earnings

What I Recommend for Tradespeople

  • ACC Cover Plus Extra: Sets a guaranteed weekly compensation amount for accidents, providing certainty over variable income
  • Income Protection: Covers illness and injuries that ACC does not. Essential when physical capacity is everything
  • Trauma Cover: Lump sum for serious health conditions diagnosed outside of an accident context
  • Life Insurance: Ensures your family is protected if the worst happens on or off the job

The key point: ACC is a starting point, not a complete solution. I work with tradespeople to build a package that genuinely covers the full range of risks, not just the ones ACC was designed for.
