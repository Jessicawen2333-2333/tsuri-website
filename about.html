My Story

I did not just study insurance.
I lived it from the inside out.

My career in the New Zealand life insurance industry began in 2012, when I joined Sovereign as a Claims Specialist. From day one, I was working directly with clients going through some of the most difficult moments of their lives — managing Disability Income claims, assessing policy entitlements, and making sure every decision was handled with both accuracy and care.

Over the years I worked my way through claims assessment, financial case management, and operations across some of New Zealand's most respected insurers. At Fidelity Life, New Zealand's largest locally owned life insurer, I was appointed Head of Operations Finance, overseeing the financial processes that underpinned the entire claims function. My most recent role at Chubb Life saw me leading the Claims Team and then stepping into the Claims Operations Manager position, where I oversaw Life, Trauma, and Income Protection claims operations at a senior level.

That is what brought me here. Not because I wanted to leave a career I was good at, but because I kept seeing the same gap. Good people. Wrong cover. No one at their side when it mattered. I knew I could change that.

Why I Do This

Insurance should protect people.
Not confuse them.

Over the years, I have reviewed thousands of claims. I have also seen what happens when a family is underinsured, or when someone has cover that simply does not apply to their situation. It is not just a financial gap. It is a promise that was never properly made.

As a Chartered Accountant, I bring a layer of financial rigour that most advisers cannot offer. If you are a business owner, I can speak to the numbers behind your policy and your business without needing to pass you on to someone else. Everything sits in one conversation, with one person who understands the full picture.

And as someone who has led claims teams and signed off on the decisions that determined whether clients received their payouts, I know exactly what your policy needs to say, and what your claim needs to look like, long before you ever need to make one.

That is the foundation of Tsuri. Built on strength. Backed by care.
